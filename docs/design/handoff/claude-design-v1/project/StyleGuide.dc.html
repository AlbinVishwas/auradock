Shape and spacing
Cards 24dp radius, sheets 28dp top, pills full. 4dp grid, 20dp screen margins, 48dp touch targets. Flat: no shadows, gradients, glows or blur surfaces.

Motion

Calm and physical. Springs settle without overshoot. Nothing bouncy.

Sheets and cards
Soft spring, damping 0.9, stiffness 380 (about 320ms). Scrim fades to 60% black. Drag to dismiss follows the finger 1:1.
Blur to focus
New content: blur 12 to 0, opacity 0 to 1, 4dp rise, 260ms, ease cubic(0.2, 0, 0, 1). Lists stagger 40ms.
Dock now morph
The pill expands into the full screen (container transform, 450ms emphasized). The thumbnail clock scales into the dock clock. System bars hide at 60%.
Task tick
Circle fills 120ms, strike-through draws left to right 220ms, text fades to #8A8A8A. After 600ms the row eases to the bottom. Light haptic tick.
Carousels
Snap on a critically damped spring. Neighbours sit at 88% scale and 40% opacity, interpolated with scroll position.
Dock clock
Digits change with a 300ms blur crossfade. Notification strip rises 12dp while the clock scales to 84%. Clock drifts up to 3dp a minute for OLED burn-in.
